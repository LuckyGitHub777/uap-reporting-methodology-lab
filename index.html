One small example of a denominator problem

Ten reports. Two very different contexts.

In this hypothetical exercise, group A produces six submitted reports from twenty observation opportunities, while group B produces four reports from five opportunities. Comparing counts without denominators hides crucial context.

Count comparison only

Six reports versus four does not establish that group A had more events. Reporting opportunity, incentives and selection can differ.
